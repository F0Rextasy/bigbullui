# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in bigbullui a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be welcoming, patient, and respectful in issues, PRs, and discussions
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully; focus on what is best for the project
- No harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or publishing others private information

## Enforcement

Violations may result in warning, temporary ban, or permanent removal at maintainer discretion. Report issues to the maintainers via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/F0Rextasy/bigbullui/issues) or the contact email on the maintainer profile.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
